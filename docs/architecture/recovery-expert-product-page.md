# Emerald Recovery Expert Product Page Architecture

## Scope

Task 5.7 replaces the `/recovery-expert` placeholder with a production product detail page for the public subscription Emerald Recovery Expert. The page describes the product role, assisted workflow, platform availability, and claim boundaries without adding pricing, checkout, downloads, authentication, or platform detail pages.

## Product Role

Emerald Recovery Expert is the assisted execution product in the Emerald product ecosystem. It is separate from the signal products, the Scanner, and the private Emerald Quant System.

## Trader-First Entry Principle

The trader manually initiates the first trade. Emerald Recovery Expert begins its management role only after the initial position has been opened by the trader.

## Assisted Execution Model

The page presents the workflow conceptually:

1. Trader chooses opportunity
2. Trader opens first trade
3. Recovery Expert monitors trade state
4. Recovery logic manages subsequent actions
5. Sequence is managed toward the configured objective

## Recovery Objective Boundary

Recovery Expert is described as designed to manage subsequent recovery actions with the objective of recovering accumulated losses and pursuing the configured profit objective. The page does not claim that recovery, profit, breakeven, or any target is certain.

## Claim-Safety Rules

The page must not claim guaranteed outcomes, risk removal, certified recovery, audited performance, broker verification, or performance attribution. Recovery logic can involve additional market exposure and does not eliminate the risk of further loss.

## Product vs Quant System

Recovery Expert is semi-automated, trader-first-entry, and public subscription. Emerald Quant System is fully automated, private investor, and governed by broader proprietary execution logic. The comparison avoids profitability claims.

## Platform Availability

Recovery Expert uses the canonical product catalog platform matrix and is modeled across MT4, MT5, TradingView, and NinjaTrader. Product-level availability does not imply identical UI, settings, workflow, broker behavior, or implementation details.

## Placeholder Asset Strategy

The page uses the registered `recovery-expert-placeholder` asset as a temporary conceptual workflow preview. It is not presented as a final EA screenshot or live product interface.

## Future Real Screenshot Replacement

The page structure supports later replacement with a real control panel, first-entry interface, recovery-state UI, or management dashboard without changing the route architecture.

## Performance Attribution Boundary

No Emerald Ledger records, Quant KPIs, signal screenshots, or public performance metrics are attached to Recovery Expert on this page.

## Deferred Implementation Details

Deferred items include exact recovery algorithm, exact lot-sizing logic, exact sequence rules, exact closure conditions, exact risk controls, exact platform UI differences, final product screenshots, subscription pricing, and download flow.
